# Contributing

Contributions are welcome: corrections, clearer workflows, extra docs, and additional fictional examples.

## Ground rules

- **No real people in examples.** Worked examples must be fully fictional. Never commit reference photos, chat logs, or personal facts about a real person.
- **Keep the method general.** Write what transfers to the next persona, not one project's diary.
- **Corrections are rules.** If a workflow step failed in practice, say what failed and what replaced it, and note it in the CHANGELOG.

## How to propose a change

1. Open an issue describing the problem or the improvement.
2. Fork the repo and make the change on a branch.
3. Keep edits small and focused, and update the CHANGELOG.
4. Open a pull request explaining what changed and how you verified it.
