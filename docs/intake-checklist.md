# Intake checklist

Collect everything in this list before generating anything. Weak intake is the top cause of likeness drift later.

## Photos

- [ ] One clear close-up of the face, front-facing, good light, minimal filter.
- [ ] One full-body shot that shows true proportions.
- [ ] Three or more everyday photos with different expressions (neutral, smiling, annoyed, sleepy).
- [ ] One photo of any signature outfit or signature item (jewelry, glasses, a trademark look).
- [ ] Reject: heavy beauty filters, group photos where the face is small, screenshots with UI covering the face.

## Videos

- [ ] At least one short clip of the person talking or moving naturally.
- [ ] Record the usage rule for videos: generation reference, or study-only.

## Speaking samples

- [ ] Chat screenshots or message logs showing real conversations, ideally 50+ messages.
- [ ] Note the basics from the samples: typical message length, bubbles per reply, catchphrases, forms of address, emoji habits, topics they start on their own.

## Voice (optional)

- [ ] One clean voice sample if voice messages are wanted.
- [ ] Record the decision if voice is skipped: text-only is a valid end state.

## Life facts

- [ ] Daily schedule with times (wake, work or classes, meals, evening routine, sleep).
- [ ] Key places: home layout, workplace or school, regular hangouts.
- [ ] Close people and how the person addresses the user.
- [ ] Recurring states tied to the schedule: what makes them tired, rushed, happy, or free.

## Boundaries

- [ ] A never-fabricate list: specifics the persona must deflect instead of inventing (names, employers, platforms, addresses, family details).
- [ ] Agreed contact hours for proactive messages, and the one chat they should arrive in.
